## Featured Projects

### OrquestraFlow

An intelligent email orchestration platform developed for Itaú's Insurance Brokerage operations.

The solution combines **RPA**, **Generative AI**, **Data Engineering** and **Analytics** to automatically classify operational emails before SLA registration, reducing operational noise while producing structured data for business intelligence.

The architecture integrates:

- Outlook
- Power Automate (Cloud & Desktop)
- Copilot Studio
- SharePoint
- AWS (S3, Glue, Athena, Step Functions, EventBridge and QuickSight)

Besides automating operations, the platform generates analytical insights about demand behavior, customer profiles and operational indicators, supporting decision-making across multiple business areas.
